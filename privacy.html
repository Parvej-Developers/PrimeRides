Return, Refund & Terms

🔁 Return Policy
  • Return vehicles by the agreed date and time at the specified location.
  • Return the vehicle in the same condition as rented, excluding normal wear and tear.
  • Late returns incur extra charges per the hourly/daily rate.
  • Excessive dirt may result in a cleaning fee.
  • Refill fuel to the original level, or refueling charges will apply.
  • Damage, missing parts, or loss will be charged as per actual repair or replacement cost.
  • Inspection with our representative is recommended at return.
💸 Refund Policy
  • Full refund for cancellations at least 48 hours before pickup.
  • 25% fee for cancellations within 48 hours of pickup.
  • No refunds for cancellations after scheduled pickup or no-shows.
  • Modifications subject to price and availability.
  • If the vehicle is unavailable, a full refund or replacement will be offered.
  • Refunds processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.
For any questions or special cases, please contact our support team.

  • Booking & Payment: Full payment before trip; prices exclude tolls, fuel, and late fees.
  • Vehicle Usage: Use for personal purposes only; no illegal or commercial use; keep it clean.
  • Fuel Policy: Return with the same fuel level as received; missing fuel will be charged at market rates.
  • Damage & Accidents: Renter bears responsibility for damages; must inform company immediately after incidents.
  • Late Return: Grace period of 30 minutes; beyond that, hourly or daily rates apply.
  • Cancellation & Refund: Full refund if canceled 24 hours before pickup; otherwise, fees may apply.
  • Company Rights: Company may refuse or cancel bookings due to unavailability or misuse.
  • Customer Responsibility: Follow all traffic rules; fines or violations are customer’s responsibility.
  • Agreement: By booking, you agree to all these terms and conditions.
Please review all conditions carefully before confirming your booking.
